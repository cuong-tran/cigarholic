All notable changes to Cigarholic. Format: [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/); entries name their feature IDs from `docs/FEATURES.md`. Releases are Git tags deployed to production after the owner's approval.

## [Unreleased]

### Changed

- OPS-07: **two-factor is no longer required on staging** (owner request). The staging deploy sets `CIGARHOLIC_FEATURES=two-factor:off` in the staging `.env`, so `staging.cigarholic.com` shows no two-factor deadline and never restricts an account to its profile. The Two Factor plugin stays active (an enrolled account is still asked for its code). Production is unchanged: two-factor stays required for administrators and shop managers.

### Added

- CNT-01 / NAV-04 / FND-02: **sample photos for every image on the homepage and for the seven sample products**, until the owner's photos arrive (OWNER-ACTIONS B9).
  - **Homepage:** the hero (walnut desktop humidor, lid open, on the Forest ground), the six category cards (desktop, cabinet and travel humidors, cigar boxes, accessories, gift sets) and the workshop panel (a walnut humidor box in clamps on the bench) replace the SVG placeholders. WebP, 15 to 60 KB each; each alt text describes what the image shows. No cigars or tobacco appear in any image.

  - **How they are made:** `scripts/sample-photos/` renders every image from code with three.js in headless Chromium: procedural wood grain (walnut, mahogany, white oak, acacia, cherry, Spanish cedar), lacquer, brass, glass and felt, softbox lighting, soft shadows and depth of field from averaging 48 jittered frames. `node scripts/sample-photos/render.mjs` rebuilds them all (about 25 minutes in the sandbox). three.js 0.187.0 is a dev dependency only; nothing new is loaded by the site.

- SHP-04 / ORD-03: **tracking numbers and the shipping email.** The manager enters the carrier's tracking number on the order. That uses WooCommerce's own Order Fulfillments: the panel opened from the fulfillment badge in the orders list or on the order page. "Find info" recognises USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL and other carriers and fills in the tracking link (77 carriers in all). No plugin is added; the plugin budget stays at 11 of 15.
  - **Provisioning:** step 50 switches the feature on (`woocommerce_feature_fulfillments_enabled`; WooCommerce 11.2 has no settings screen for it). Step 70 enables its three customer emails: shipped, shipment updated, shipment removed.
  - **`Shipping\Tracking`** (toggle `tracking`, on) makes the customer's "Fulfillment created" email the store's shipping email.
    - **Subject:** "Your Cigarholic order 1234 has shipped", or "Part of your …" while items are still to ship and "The rest of your …" for the last package.
    - **Content:** the carrier's name (WooCommerce printed its key, "usps"), the tracking number and a **Track your package** button. The button appears only when there is an http(s) link. For customers with an account, a link to the order in it.
    - **WooCommerce's wording replaced:** "Woo! Some items you purchased are being fulfilled", an empty "Tracking Number" line and an empty link when no tracking was entered, and "My Account > Orders" links for guests that pointed to `/wp/my-account/orders/`, a 404 on Bedrock.
    - **Templates:** the four templates (HTML and plain text of the email and of the shipment block) are served from `cigarholic-core/templates/`; a theme override would still win.
    - A subject or heading set in WooCommerce → Settings → Emails is kept.
  - **One action ships an order:** a processing order whose items have all shipped is completed. The "Completed order" email is not sent for such an order, because the shipping email already went out; an order completed without a shipment still gets it. On-hold orders are never completed by a shipment.
  - **Customer's order page:** "It has shipped." / "Part of it has shipped." instead of WooCommerce's "It has been Fulfilled." and "It has no fulfillments yet."
  - Manager guide section "Shipping an order: tracking number and shipping email".
  - **Tests:**

### Security

- FND-08 (#39): `wp-cli.yml` loads the owner email and initial password from secrets only, and only for a command that references them; `infra-provision.yml` reads `LETSENCRYPT_EMAIL` from the secret only. A repository variable's value is printed in the logs of this public repository.
- OPS-07 / FND-08 (#36, 2026-11-02): the staging basic-auth exemptions for payment webhooks matched URI prefixes, so `/wp/wp-admin/install.php?wc-api=x` and `/wc-api/../wp/wp-admin/install.php` reached the WordPress installer of the freshly deployed, not yet installed staging site without credentials (verified from outside, HTTP 200).
